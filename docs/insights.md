1. **Finding:** Price predicts performance only moderately (rank correlation 0.52; 0.63 in
   2022, 0.30 in 2024).
   **Caveat:** expensive players also play more games; 2023-24 samples are small.
   **So what:** a high price mostly buys reliability, not a guarantee.

2. **Finding:** Points per player rise about 6x from the cheapest band to the 10+ crore band,
   while the price rises about 37x.
   **Caveat:** rough score; cheap players are mostly bench players.
   **So what:** stars have sharply diminishing returns on price alone.

3. **Finding:** Most of the best-value buys cost 1-3 crore (Conway, Kuldeep Yadav, Tilak Varma).
   **Caveat:** 13 of the top 15 are from 2022, which dominates the data.

4. **Finding:** Teams differ modestly in auction efficiency (MI 58 vs GT 40 points per crore),
   but the gap among ranks 3-10 is small.
   **Caveat:** auction buys only; retained stars are excluded; 3 seasons.
